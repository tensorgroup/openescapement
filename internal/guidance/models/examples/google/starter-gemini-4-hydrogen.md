# Gemini 4 Hydrogen governance (gemini-4-hydrogen)

- Route everyday coding, parallel worker agents, and lower-stakes review to `gemini-4-hydrogen`. It is the fastest and cheapest Gemini 4 model, and the only one on the API free tier.
- Set the thinking level explicitly. Hydrogen accepts `low`, `medium`, `high` (the default), and `xhigh`.
- In the Antigravity CLI (`agy`), select it by an effort-suffixed id, such as `gemini-4-hydrogen-high`.
- Keep planning, plan-checking, and review of high-stakes changes on `gemini-4-neon`. Run bulk and mechanical work on `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`.
- Google names it the upgrade for `gemini-3.8-flash` and `gemini-3.6-flash`. Migrate saved configurations to it.

See the Google model guidance page for sources and full governance notes.
