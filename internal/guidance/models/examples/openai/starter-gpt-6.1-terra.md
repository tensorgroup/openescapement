- Use `gpt-6.1-terra` for tasks that depend on visual understanding or computer use: reading screenshots and driving a user interface. OpenAI calls it an efficient model that excels at both.
- Send other day-to-day coding to `gpt-6.2-sol` and planning or review to `gpt-6.2-astra`.
- Check its price on OpenAI's pricing page before running it at scale.
